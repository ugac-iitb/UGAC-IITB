Analytics Club

Overview

The Analytics Club, IIT Bombay is one of the primary student-led bodies representing the interests of students in the fields of Data Science, Machine Learning, and Artificial Intelligence. As a hub for over 10,000 enthusiasts across campus, the club is dedicated to fostering a strong and collaborative analytics community within the institute. Our initiatives span a wide range of activities—including speaker sessions, workshops, bootcamps, hackathons, and industry-interaction programs, covering cutting-edge areas such as Machine Learning, AI, Big Data Handling, and Data Visualization. These efforts aim to make analytics more accessible, engaging, and relevant to students from all academic backgrounds. With a clear focus on skill-building and awareness, the club strives to keep students informed about emerging trends and career opportunities in analytics. Whether you're just starting out or looking to deepen your knowledge, the Analytics Club offers a vibrant platform to explore, learn, and grow in the ever-evolving world of data science and AI/ML
